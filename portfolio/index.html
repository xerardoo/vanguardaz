    Questions builders ask us.

    How long from signed quote to installed windows?

    After you sign, we pre-walk the job to verify every opening. Fabrication takes 2 to 4 weeks from there, and we book your install day to the manufacturer's delivery date.

    Do you supply the windows, install them, or both?

    Both. We quote product and labor together, so one company owns the result from the order to the last bead of sealant.

    What do you need to give me a quote?

    The plans, ideally with the window and door schedule. If you don't have them handy, a list of opening sizes works to get started.

    My budget for windows is fixed. Can you work with that?

    Yes, and it's one of the most common conversations we have. Tell us the number and we'll re-quote with a different product line or frame material. The installation stays the same.

    How does payment work?

    A 50% deposit when you sign the final quote, which is what lets us order your windows. The balance is due as outlined in your quote.

    What does your warranty cover?

    Our installation warranty covers water leaks, air leaks and windows or doors that don't operate correctly because of the install. You get the full terms in writing with your quote.

    Why do you measure again if I already sent plans?

    Because framing never matches the plans to the inch. The pre-walk catches the difference before a window is built to the wrong size, not after it shows up on site.

    Do you work on commercial and multi-family projects?

    Yes. We install in new homes and new buildings. Send the plans and we'll tell you straight away if it's a fit.

    Where do you work?

    Across the Phoenix metro, with most of our work in the West Valley: Avondale, Goodyear, Litchfield Park, Buckeye, Glendale and Peoria.
