    Questions homeowners ask us.

    Will my house be a mess?

    No. That's the whole point of our Clean Promise. We cover your floors and furniture, take everything with us, and walk the house with you before we leave.

    How long does it take?

    Your windows are made to fit your home, which takes 2 to 4 weeks after you order. We schedule your install day as soon as they're ready.

    I don't know anything about windows. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. Tell us what bothers you about the ones you have (too hot, hard to open, noisy) and we'll recommend what fits your home and your budget, in plain English.

    What if something goes wrong later?

    Our installation comes with a written warranty. If a window leaks water or air, or doesn't open and close the way it should, call us and we come back.

    How do I pay?

    A 50% deposit when you approve your quote, so we can order your windows. The rest is due as written in your quote.

    Where do you work?

    Avondale, Goodyear, Litchfield Park, Buckeye, Glendale, Peoria and the rest of the Phoenix area.
